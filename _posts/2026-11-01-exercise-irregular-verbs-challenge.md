There is no way around irregular verbs — English kept too many of its oldest verbs for that. But there is a way to make them *less* painful: notice that they travel in families.

| Family | Members |
| --- | --- |
| **-ought / -aught** | buy → bought, bring → brought, think → thought, catch → caught, teach → taught |
| **i → a → e** | give → gave → given, take → took → taken, sing → sang → sung |
| **deep → kept** | keep → kept, sleep → slept, leave → left, feel → felt |

Learn one member of a family, and you effectively learn the whole dinner table.
