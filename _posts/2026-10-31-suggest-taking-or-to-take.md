exercise:
  title: "Choose the natural sentence"
  question: "Which sentence uses suggest naturally?"
  choices:
    - "She suggested to take a short break."
    - "She suggested taking a short break."
    - "She suggested us to take a short break."
  answer: 1
---
Some English verbs are followed by a particular form. **Suggest** is commonly followed by a noun or an **-ing** form: *She suggested a short break* or *She suggested taking a short break.*

It is not normally followed by *to + verb* in the patterns above. To name the person who should act, you can use **suggest that**: *She suggested that we take a short break.*

A useful way to remember the pattern is to collect a few examples rather than translate word for word: *suggest going*, *suggest a change*, *suggest that we wait*.
