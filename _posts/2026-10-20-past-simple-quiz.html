The classic dilemma! Choose the correct verb form in each sentence. You'll get instant feedback — try to beat your score on a second run.

Remember the rule

  • Past simple — finished time: yesterday, last week, in 2019, ago. The time is closed.
  • Present perfect — unfinished time or no time at all: ever, never, just, already, yet, since, for. The connection to now is open.
