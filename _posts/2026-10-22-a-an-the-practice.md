exercise:
  title: "Choose the best fit"
  question: "Complete the sentence: I have ___ apple, but ___ apple is for you."
  choices:
    - "a / an"
    - "an / the"
    - "the / a"
  answer: 1
---
Use **a** or **an** when you introduce one nonspecific countable noun: *a book, an idea*. The choice depends on the first **sound**, not the first letter: *an hour*, but *a university*.

Use **the** when the speaker and listener can identify which person or thing you mean. In the sentence in the exercise, the first mention introduces an apple; the second refers back to that same apple, so the listener now knows which one.

English articles have more details and exceptions, but this first-mention / known-mention contrast is a useful place to begin.
