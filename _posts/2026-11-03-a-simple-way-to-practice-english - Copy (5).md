A short daily routine is often more effective than a long weekly study session.

## Start with five minutes

Read one short paragraph aloud. Circle two useful phrases, then write one sentence with each phrase.

## Make it interactive

Try turning the paragraph into a quick exercise: remove the target phrases and fill them in again from memory.

Consistency matters more than intensity.
