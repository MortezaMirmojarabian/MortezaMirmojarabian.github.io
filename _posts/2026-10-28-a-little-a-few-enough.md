The choice between **a little** and **a few** becomes easier when you look at the noun that follows.

## Count the noun

Use **a few** with plural countable nouns: *a few questions, a few minutes,* and *a few ideas*. Use **a little** with uncountable nouns: *a little water, a little time,* and *a little patience*.

**Enough** works with both: *enough chairs* and *enough space*. Put it before the noun, or after an adjective: *enough room; warm enough*.

The expression *a few* usually sounds more positive than *few*: *I have a few minutes* suggests there is some time; *I have few minutes* emphasizes that there is not much.

## Listen for the noun

When you are unsure, ask whether the noun is countable in this sentence. Then choose the phrase that agrees with it. A little attention to the noun is often enough.
