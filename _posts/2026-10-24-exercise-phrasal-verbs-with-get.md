Phrasal verbs with **get** are everywhere in natural English — conversations, podcasts, series, emails. And they are exactly the kind of vocabulary that refuses to be learned by translation, because each combination creates a meaning you cannot guess from its parts.

The strategy that works: learn each phrasal verb as a **whole chunk**, attached to one vivid, personal situation. *Last Monday I couldn't get through to the dentist — the line was busy all morning.* One sentence, and the word has a home.

Below you will meet eight of the most useful *get* verbs. Type the missing verb in each gap — the meaning hint sits in brackets so you always know what you are reaching for. Then press **Check answers** for your score and an explanation for every item.
