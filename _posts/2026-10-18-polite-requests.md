There is no single phrase that makes every request polite. Tone, context, and your relationship with the listener all matter.

## Choose a phrase for the moment

With a colleague, **Could you send me the notes when you have a moment?** is clear and considerate. In a busy shop, **Could I have a receipt, please?** is ordinary and polite. With a friend, **Can you pass me that book?** may sound perfectly natural.

**Would you mind…?** is another option, but remember that it is followed by an **-ing** form: *Would you mind opening the window?* A short **please** can help, but adding several softeners may make a simple request feel less direct than you intend.

## Be clear and kind

Try saying what you need, give the other person a little room where appropriate, and use a tone that fits the conversation. Natural politeness is usually clear rather than elaborate.
