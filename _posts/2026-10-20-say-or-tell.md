Both **say** and **tell** are used when we report words, but their sentence patterns are different.

## Tell + person

We normally use *tell* with the person who receives the information:

- She **told me** the truth.
- Can you **tell us** your name?
- I **told him** that I was busy.

Don't say *She told that she was busy* when you mean to report what she said. Include the person, or use *say*.

## Say + words / say something to someone

Use *say* before the words or message:

- He **said** hello.
- They **said** that they were ready.
- She **said something to** the manager.

Compare:

- He **told me** he was tired.
- He **said to me** that he was tired.

The first is usually the more natural everyday choice. Remember the core patterns: **tell someone something** and **say something to someone**.

## Quick speaking practice

Think of a time when someone gave you good advice. What did they tell you? What exactly did they say?
