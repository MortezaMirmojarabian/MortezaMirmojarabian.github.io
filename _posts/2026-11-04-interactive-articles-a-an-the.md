*A*, *an*, and *the* are the three most common words in English, and some of the trickiest. The basic idea: *a/an* introduces something new and general, *the* points at something specific that both speaker and listener can identify. Try the quiz, then read the notes below if any answer surprises you.

## The three rules to remember

1. **A/an** = one of many, first mention, any one will do. Choose by the next *sound*, not the next letter.
2. **The** = we both know which one: already mentioned, unique, or obvious from context.
3. **No article** = general ideas (music, honesty), meals, languages, and most names of people, cities, and single mountains.
