Two tenses do most of the daily work in English: the **present simple** and the **present continuous**. The division of labor is quite tidy:

| Tense | Job | Signal words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present simple | habits, routines, facts, timetables | usually, always, every day, often |
| Present continuous | happening now, temporary periods, arranged plans | now, at the moment, this week, today |

One extra rule worth more than all the others: **stative verbs** — *know, like, want, hear, believe, understand* — describe states, not actions, so they almost never wear the continuous form. *"I am knowing"* is the classic giveaway.
