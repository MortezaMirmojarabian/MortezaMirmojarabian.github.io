**Borrow** and **lend** describe the same exchange from opposite directions.

- The person who receives something **borrows** it: *May I borrow your pen?*
- The person who gives something **lends** it: *I can lend you my pen.*

You can picture the object moving **to the borrower** and **from the lender**. The common structures are *borrow something from someone* and *lend something to someone* (or *lend someone something*).

For example: *I borrowed a book from Neda. Neda lent me the book.* If you pause over the sentence, ask whose hands the object is moving toward; that usually points you to the right verb.
