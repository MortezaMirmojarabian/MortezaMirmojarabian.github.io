Articles are small words that cause big headaches. This short exercise covers the four choices you make every day: **a**, **an**, **the**, and **no article at all**.

## Quick reminders

- Use **a / an** for one of many, or the first time you mention something. Choose between them by **sound**, not spelling: *an hour*, *a university*.
- Use **the** when both people know which one you mean, or when there is only one.
- Use **no article** with meals (*have breakfast*) and with general plurals and uncountable nouns (*I like music*).
