About

Hi, I'm Morteza — an English teacher and web developer. I teach at the Iran Language Institute (ILI), where I like finding clearer ways to explain how English works, and ways to make practice feel less like homework.

I'm a web enthusiast at heart, so I build small web tools, like an online HTML editor, and I manage a shared digital library for my fellow teachers. This site is where it all comes together: articles on the English language, interactive exercises, and the projects I'm working on.
