About

A little about me

Hello! I’m Morteza, an English teacher at the ILI (Iran Language Institute) with a soft spot for grammar, vocabulary, and the small — but stubborn — details of the English language. Most of my week is spent in classrooms, helping learners move from “I am agree” to “I agree”, one clear explanation at a time.

When I’m not teaching, I build things for the web. I believe well-designed tools and exercises make learning faster and a little more fun, so on this site I combine both: short, practical articles followed by interactive exercises that give you instant feedback.

Have a look around, try an exercise, and if something still doesn’t make sense — tell me. That’s usually how the next post gets written.
