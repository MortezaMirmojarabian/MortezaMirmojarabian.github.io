I'm an English teacher and a lifelong language nerd who loves turning tricky grammar into plain, friendly explanations. By day I teach, design practice activities, and curate resources for my students; by night I build small web tools that make learning and teaching a little easier.

This site is where I share what I know: short, practical articles on English grammar, vocabulary and usage; interactive exercises you can complete right in your browser; and the occasional project announcement. Everything here is free, and always will be.

When I'm not teaching or writing, I'm probably reading, drinking too much tea, or tweaking this very website.
